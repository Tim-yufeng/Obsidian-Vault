Here's our key partners:
Our key partner are universities and departments, including career centers, which give us credibility and help us reach students. Student clubs and societies can provide early users and real campus tasks, like poster design or video editing.

We also need startups and small companies as project providers. They often have real tasks but may not have the budget to hire full-time workers. Payment and legal support partners can make the transaction safer and more legally protected. HR teams can later use verified project records to discover student talent.

For key resources, we see three stages. The first stage is the trust network, which we have just introduced. The second stage is the operating platform, including matching algorithms, task verification, payment protection, ratings, and certification. Third is our database, including student profiles,  project records, ratings, and employer feedback.

In short, SkillBridge’s resources grow from trust, to systems, and finally to data advantage.
